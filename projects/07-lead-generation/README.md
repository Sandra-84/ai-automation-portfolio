# Lead Generation & AI Qualification

> AI conversations become structured, qualified and measurable lead data.

## Challenge

Potential customers interact with AI, but valuable lead information can remain inside the conversation.

## Solution

An AI Agent answers questions using a RAG knowledge base, identifies potential leads and automatically submits structured lead data to the business.

## How it works

User → AI Agent → RAG / Qdrant → Intent Analysis → Lead Qualification → Submit Lead

Lead data can be routed to Google Sheets, Gmail or Telegram via Webhooks.

## Capabilities

- AI-powered customer conversations
- RAG-based knowledge retrieval
- Intent analysis
- Lead qualification
- Structured lead submission
- Automated lead delivery

## Stack

n8n · AI Agent · Qdrant · RAG · OpenRouter · Google Sheets · Webhooks · Gmail · Telegram
