# Lead Generation & AI Qualification

## AI-Powered Lead Generation

An AI workflow that transforms conversations into structured, qualified and measurable lead data.

## Problem

Lead information coming from conversations needs to be identified, structured and qualified before it can be delivered to the appropriate business channels.

## Solution

An AI Agent analyzes the conversation, identifies intent and qualifies the lead.

Relevant information is structured and submitted through webhooks to business communication and data channels.

## Workflow

AI Conversation
↓
AI Agent
↓
Intent Analysis
↓
Lead Qualification
↓
Structured Lead Data
↓
Webhook
↓
Google Sheets / Gmail / Telegram
